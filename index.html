Jake (full title: Jacob "Jake" the Dog, Sr.) is the deuteragonist of Adventure Time. He is a dog/shape-shifter hybrid, referred to by others as a "magic dog". He is Finn's constant companion, best friend, and adoptive brother. At various points in the series, Jake has been shown to be a very proficient chef who regularly experiments with new techniques and dishes.

    Prepare the ingredients

  1. Season the meat with rosemary, thyme, pepper, and salt. Vacuum-seal and sous-vide until tender and safely cooked. Sear briefly and slice thinly.
  2. Boil, peel, and dice the eggs.
  3. Slice the cucumbers, tomatoes, and onions.
  4. Fry the bacon until crispy.
  5. Cook the poultry separately until fully done.
  6. Chop the dill and pickles.

    7. Prepare the baguette

  7. Split the baguette lengthwise and brush with olive oil.
  8. Torch the cut sides until golden and crispy. Let cool slightly.

    9. Assemble the sandwich

  9. Layer the bottom half of the baguette with:
    1. A generous layer of cream cheese.
    2. Chopped pickles and fresh dill.
    3. Diced boiled eggs.
    4. Cooked poultry.
    5. Sliced cucumbers and Roma tomatoes.
    6. Thinly sliced sweet yellow onions.
    7. A few drops of safely collected tears, or sea salt as a substitute.
    8. Generous slices of rosemary-and-thyme sous-vide meat.
    9. Crispy bacon.
